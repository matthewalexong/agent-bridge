# Code of conduct

Agent Bridge is built through evidence, respectful disagreement, and careful stewardship of users' browser privacy. Everyone participating in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and community spaces is expected to help maintain a safe and constructive environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient with people of different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Welcome good-faith questions, corrections, and reproducible negative results.
- Respect privacy. Never pressure someone to disclose credentials, account data, private browsing content, identity, or other unnecessary personal information.
- Disclose relevant conflicts, incentives, affiliations, or access when making product, security, or architectural claims.
- Accept moderation decisions and make a good-faith effort to repair harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, impersonation, deliberate misinformation, credential solicitation, publishing private browser artifacts, and sustained personal attacks are not tolerated. Technical disagreement does not excuse hostile or exclusionary conduct.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an immediate public-space problem, stop engaging and flag the content through GitHub's moderation controls. For a confidential report, use this repository's private **Report a vulnerability** channel and begin the title with `Conduct report:`. Do not include more private information than the maintainers need to understand the incident.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, limit participation, or permanently exclude a participant based on severity, pattern, and risk to the community. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, with conflicts of interest avoided when possible. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is itself a violation.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing Agent Bridge. It does not control unrelated private conduct, but behavior that creates a credible safety risk for project participants may affect access to project spaces.
