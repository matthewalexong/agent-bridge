# Candidate discovery coverage target

This target prevents a product ranker from confidently choosing among a narrow,
sponsored, affiliate-heavy, wrong-market, or otherwise indefensible shortlist.

The harness must:

1. bind the discovery plan to the exact category and destination market;
2. enumerate required discovery lanes and justify every skipped lane;
3. require fresh, verified, completed searches for every required lane;
4. require genuinely distinct query text and query families;
5. retain direct-page provenance rather than treating snippets as evidence;
6. canonicalize product candidates and separate exact, unavailable, rejected,
   and unresolved candidates;
7. count source independence separately from corporate ownership diversity;
8. distinguish independent, brand, seller, sponsored, affiliate, and unknown
   commercial relationships;
9. prevent sponsored and affiliate placements from manufacturing source
   independence;
10. require enough viable candidates and independent sources for the declared
    bounded plan;
11. pass only cleared candidate IDs into evidence aggregation and preference
    ranking;
12. require a fresh candidate-coverage dossier artifact whenever products were
    discovered or shortlisted;
13. state that bounded plan sufficiency is not world- or market-exhaustiveness;
    and
14. never rank, select, authorize checkout, or authorize purchase.

Hermes or OpenClaw remains the main brain. Gemma may normalize already captured
URLs, domains, exact query text, and canonical identifiers. Gemma must not judge
lane applicability, market or candidate scope, source independence, ownership,
sponsorship, coverage sufficiency, or product choice.
